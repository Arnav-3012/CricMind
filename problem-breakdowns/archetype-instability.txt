BIG_PICTURE: The data has no discrete player types at this size. Silhouette < 0.25 everywhere = "no substantial structure" (Kaufman-Rousseeuw scale). With seed ARI ~0.99 but boot ARI ~0.4, the fit is fully repeatable on fixed data but the boundaries move when the data is resampled: k-means is cutting a continuum, and cuts through dense regions are unstable by construction (Ben-David & von Luxburg 2008). Embeddings carry almost no player style (Phase 3 probe ratio < 0.2, D-043), so their clusters are noise (ARI ~0). No feature or method fix creates types that are not there; fixes can only make a partition of a continuum more repeatable.
